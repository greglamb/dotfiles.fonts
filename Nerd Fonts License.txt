The icon, powerline and braille glyphs in the MesloLGS NF DF faces are put
there by the Nerd Fonts font-patcher (https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts,
v3.5.1, run with --complete). They come from the glyph sets below, each under
its own license, as listed in Nerd Fonts' src/glyphs/README.md and in the
license files that ship with each set. The license of the faces as a whole is
in "MesloLGS NF DF License.txt".
