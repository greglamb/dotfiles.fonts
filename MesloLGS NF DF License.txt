MesloLGS NF DF is Meslo LG S, patched by Nerd Fonts, with gaps filled from the
Noto fonts. It is distributed as a whole under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1 (full text in "Noto License.txt"). It contains glyphs copied from
fonts under that license -- the Noto fonts, and the Font Awesome, Pomicons and
Weather Icons sets Nerd Fonts patches in -- and the OFL requires a font built
with such glyphs to be distributed under it (OFL condition 5, OFL FAQ 3.2).

Each part keeps its own copyright and license as well:

  Meslo LG S: the Latin text, the metrics      Apache License 2.0 (below)
  Nerd Fonts: icons, powerline, braille        "Nerd Fonts License.txt"
  Noto: symbols and emoji the fill adds        SIL OFL 1.1, "Noto License.txt"
